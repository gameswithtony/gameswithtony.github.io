Important Notes

  • The AIs play by exactly the same rules as you: the same cards, money, income, coal, iron and beer. They have no special rules.
  • The app will refer to spaces in locations by number, for example "Coalbrookdale (Space 2)". The spaces are numbered from left-to-right, top-to-bottom. See the below reference:
  • The app keeps track of everyone's money and income, including yours. Tell it what you do, and it will tell you how much money you have.
  • If you think the app is making a mistake, use the 'Game State' link at the top of the app to compare your physical board with the app's. 'History' shows every move so far.
  • If you load the app in your device's browser the app will work and save if you turn on airplane mode. You will need an internet connection to refresh the page however.
